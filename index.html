About the Artist

Born from
Darkness,
Forged in Sound

VOIDEX emerged from the underground electronic scene in 2017, blending cinematic textures, pulsing bass frequencies, and haunting melodic architecture into a sound that defies categorization.

Performing across three continents, each live set is a ritualistic experience — light, smoke, and sub-bass converging into something visceral and unforgettable.
